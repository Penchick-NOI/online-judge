# Organizations: approving requests

An **organization** on PNOI is a group of students, such as a school club or a training group. Its page ranks its
members. Students cannot create one directly: they send a request, and someone on the PNOI team approves or
rejects it. This guide is for the team member doing that.

## What the student does

1. On the **Organizations** page (under **Users**), the student clicks **Start an organization**.
2. They fill in the name, one or two sentences about what it is for, and whether **Anyone can join**.
3. They click **Send request**. The Organizations page then tells them the request is waiting.

A student can have only one request waiting at a time, and the name cannot match an existing organization.

## What you do

Your account needs the permission to change **requests to start an organization** (administrators have it).

1. Open the **Organizations** page. If requests are waiting, a line at the top says how many. Click it.
   (Or open the admin pages and choose **Requests to start an organization**.)
2. Read the name and description of each request. Click a name to see it in full.
3. To **approve**: tick the request, choose **Approve: create the organization** in the **Action** box above
   the list, and click **Go**.
   - The organization appears on the site straight away.
   - The student who asked becomes its administrator and its first member.
4. To **reject**: first open the request and type a short **Reply to the student** saying why, then click
   **Save**. Back on the list, tick it, choose **Reject**, and click **Go**.
   - The student sees your reply on the Organizations page and can send a new request.
   - If you reject without a reply, the student is not told anything.

## Things to check before approving

- The name is a real club, school group or training group, not a joke or something offensive.
- It is not pretending to be an official body (for example "PNOI Team") unless it really is.
- It does not copy the name of an organization that already exists.

## After approval

- The student who asked can edit the description and manage members from the organization's page.
- You can rename or remove an organization in the admin pages under **Organizations**.
